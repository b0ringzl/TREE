    Model evidence / 模型判讀依據

    Scores come from image–text similarity; the adapted mode adds three trained MLP heads. Neither supplies verified leaf or bark traits.
    分數來自影像與文字特徵相似度；適配模式加入三個已訓練的 MLP 分類頭。兩者均不提供經驗證的葉片或樹皮特徵解釋。

    Scope & species / 適用範圍與樹種

    Image-only classification, not panorama detection or point-cloud fusion. Scores are not field accuracy; species beyond these 148 classes may be misclassified.
    僅作影像分類，並非全景偵測或點雲融合。分數並非實地正確率；不屬於這 148 類的物種仍可能誤判。

    Images are processed locally and are not retained after inference. The original Demo and its weights remain separate.
    影像僅在本機處理，推理後不予保留。原版 Demo 與其權重保持獨立。

    Ficus microcarpa and Ficus benjamina share the banyan label. The Araucaria label retains the existing merged class.
    細葉榕與垂葉榕共用榕樹標籤；南洋杉保留現有合併類別定義。
